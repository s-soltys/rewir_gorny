# Revelation and clue map — candidate only

**Status: PROVISIONAL.** This records a possible connected-mystery architecture. No clue or reveal here is approved canon until Szymon accepts it and the outline supports it.

| Clue | First reading | Later reading (candidate) | What it must not pretend to prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cable smoke precedes vivid episodes | Burning copper creates the anomaly. | Burning is a marker, trigger, or way of perceiving a spatial pattern already present. | The anomaly's ultimate cause. |

| The shadow at the clearing | An intruder/creature has entered the forest. | It may be a shape at the edge of a spatial overlap or an effect of perception. | A monster's identity or intent. |

## Reveal ladder

1. **Surface mystery:** what is happening to the people and places around the clearing?
2. **Human mystery:** who knew what, who profited, and why did they choose their strategy?
3. **Historical reframe:** the anomaly appears older than the current cable fires and institutional records are evidence of observation, not a complete explanation.
4. **Unknowable remainder:** the characters can map consequences and choose how to respond, but cannot establish what the phenomenon is or wants.

## Fairness rules

- Plant a clue before its reframe and keep it in the same document/scene ledger.
- A reveal must alter a character's next decision, not just surprise the audience.
- Avoid “it was all a dream,” a hidden mastermind with complete knowledge, or a last-minute science explanation.
- If two interpretations remain possible, show what each explains and what it fails to explain.
