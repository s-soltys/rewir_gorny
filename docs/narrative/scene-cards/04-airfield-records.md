# Scene 04 — The Airfield Records

**Status:** PROVISIONAL candidate; this scene is not in the old playable prototype.

- **Location:** Old airfield and/or a nearby records room; exact history requires research.
- **Present:** Protagonist; a proposed record-keeper/worker may appear.
- **Immediate goal:** Find evidence that can distinguish contamination, cover-up, and spatial inconsistency.
- **Conflict:** Records may be missing, contradictory, or restricted. A record-keeper may be trying to prevent harm as well as hide liability.
- **Clue:** A route/map mark or repeated description predates the current cable-burning gatherings.
- **Choice:** Copy, publish, conceal, or return the record to the person who shared it.
- **Consequence:** Faction trust shifts; the choice affects who can act on the information.
- **Open:** Whether such records exist in the story, who made them, and what they actually establish. Do not assert a historical military program.
