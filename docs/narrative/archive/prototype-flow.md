# Archived prototype flow — not current canon

**Status: ARCHIVED.** This summarizes the old Phaser/Ink demo so useful material is not lost while executable code is removed from `main`. It is not the plot outline and must not be treated as approved dialogue.

## Playable route in the old demo

1. **Childhood room:** protagonist wakes; considers a damaged Kinder-surprise robot and can take or leave it.
2. **Family flat / hallway:** Mom insists on a hat. Dad gives the protagonist a strange coin and asks them to ask about it.
3. **Street / kiosk:** a “Weirdo” says the copper is rising; showing the coin gets the claim that it wards off the hum.
4. **Forest:** the Gateway Friend arrives; a shadow detaches from the tree line. The protagonist runs or stands their ground (the toy can ground them); the shadow dissipates into copper dust.

## Useful seeds retained

- Childhood toy as memory/grounding object.
- Family obligation and the hat as ordinary, specific pressure.
- The strange coin and kiosk-side warning.
- A forest gathering and the hum.
- The Gateway Friend and the choice to stay or flee.

## Known prototype limitations

The route was short and linear; most choices converged. The cable-burning gathering itself, faction conflict, the broader friend group, and the intended reality/memory mystery were largely absent. Traits and copper exposure were declared but mostly unused. The speaker labels were plain text rather than Ink tags, and the old TTS assets did not match the dialogue. The prototype's “deep mines currency” and “coin wards off the hum” were character claims, not verified lore.

## Rule for future use

Borrow only what still serves the approved plot. Re-author the playable story after the outline and revelation map are approved.
