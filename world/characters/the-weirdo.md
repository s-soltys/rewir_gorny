# The Weirdo

**Status: PROVISIONAL.** The old prototype contains a kiosk-side figure who talks about copper and the hum; the identity and history below are working possibilities only.

- **Age:** deliberately uncertain in the draft.
- **Role in prototype:** says the copper is rising; calls the strange coin a relic and claims it wards off the hum.

## Working characterization

May have noticed spatial inconsistencies before the protagonist. Speaks in fragments and repeats phrases; this could come from fear, illness, exposure, a deliberate performance, or something else. Do not declare him a prophet or a reliable lore-delivery device.

## Story function

He gives the protagonist an interpretation that later evidence can challenge. If he is right about anything, make that rightness partial and testable; if he is wrong, show why his belief remains understandable.

## Open

Name, age, work/history, where he lives, whether he has been exposed, what the coin is, and which of his statements can be independently corroborated.
