# Factions — working model

**Status: PROVISIONAL.** These are design candidates derived from the story discussions, not approved canon. Keep the cast small; factions exist to produce conflict between defensible strategies, not to build a war-game roster.

| Group | What they want | Why their view makes sense | What they get wrong / harm caused |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Burners** — the youth group around the clearing | Keep the gathering, income, and group intact; resist losing the only place that feels theirs. | Scrap has value; the ritual predates the crisis; the anomaly is intermittent and their friends need one another. | They normalize exposure and may pressure vulnerable friends to stay. The Gateway Friend's loyalty can become coercive. |
| **The Cordon / Record-Keepers** — possible municipal, medical, or former-airfield workers | Restrict access, limit exposure, preserve or suppress reports. | Exposure appears harmful; contradictory reports make public guidance difficult; uncontrolled crowds may make it worse. | Cordon or secrecy can destroy livelihoods, isolate residents, and protect institutions more than people. Their records are partial, not an explanation. |
| **The Scrap Network** — traders and opportunists around the new market economy | Keep copper moving and earn money from a rapidly changing economy. | Scrap work offers real income where stable work is scarce; no one has proved the metal causes the event. | They can knowingly push risk onto young burners, distort provenance, and profit from the uncertainty. |

## Faction-writing test

For every major action, write all four: (1) what the actor believes, (2) what evidence they have, (3) the human good they are trying to protect, and (4) who pays the cost. Give each faction internal disagreement. No group should possess the whole truth.
