# Osiedle Street

**Status: PROVISIONAL location draft.** Confirm street names, kiosk details, market references, and period advertising through research before using them as literal history.

- **Copper exposure:** low to medium, according to the old world-bible model; exact levels are OPEN.
- **Story function:** threshold between family flat, market/kiosk, blocks, and forest edge.

## Place

Cracked pavement between concrete blocks. A kiosk can anchor chance encounters. Sodium-orange streetlights and imported used cars belong to the project's visual palette; the previous draft imagined cigarette/lottery posters, a taxi number, a barking dog, and dubbed television as background detail.

## Anomaly

Possible shimmer at the edge of sodium-lit areas; shadows may seem to have a second, trembling edge. These are observations to use selectively, not a constant effect.

## Who may be here

The kiosk-side Weirdo, neighbors, youths, market-goers, and people moving between home and the forest.

## Open

Exact kiosk and market location, street layout, specific period objects, and how much of the anomaly an unexposed passer-by notices.
